hanami

A small game launcher for Linux and Windows. It lives in the tray, finds your games by itself, starts the one you pick, and gets out of the way.

Download for Linux Download for Windows

Free and open source · about 30 MB · all releases

What it does

Finds your games
Steam, Heroic, Lutris and your app menu on Linux; Steam, Epic and GOG on Windows. Anything else goes in one settings file.
Stays out of the way
No CPU while it waits in the tray. Press Ctrl+Alt+H, type a few letters, press Enter, and it is gone again.
Explains itself
The ? in the panel copies a prompt for any AI that can see your computer. It tells you everything Hanami can do and changes nothing.

Start

  1. Download for your system and unpack it.
  2. Run hanami. A blossom appears in your tray.
  3. Open it with Ctrl+Alt+H or a click on the blossom. The guide covers the rest.
